Common Questions About Going Solar

How much does solar really cost?
With our $0 down financing, you can go solar with no upfront cost. Most homeowners save up to 60% on their electric bill — and with 6 months of zero electricity payments, you start saving from day one. Your solar specialist will provide an exact quote tailored to your home.
Will solar work on my roof type?
Yes. We install on tile, shingle, metal, and flat roofs. During your free consultation, we assess your roof condition, angle, and sun exposure to design the optimal system for your specific home.
What happens during hurricanes?
Tesla solar panels are rated for winds exceeding 130 mph. Add a Tesla Powerwall battery, and you’ll have backup power when the grid goes down. Your home stays powered through the storm while neighbors go dark.
What if I want to sell my house?
Homes with solar sell faster and for more money. Studies show a 4-6% property value increase. In Florida, the solar property tax exemption means you won’t pay additional property taxes on that added value.
How long does installation take?
Most systems are fully operational within 6-8 weeks from contract signing. The physical installation typically takes 1-3 days. The remaining time covers permitting, utility approvals, and final inspection.
Is $0 down financing really zero?
Yes. You pay nothing upfront. Financing is available with competitive rates, and many homeowners find their monthly solar payment is less than their current electric bill. Plus, you get 6 months paying zero for electricity.
Can my HOA block solar panels?
No. The Florida Solar Rights Act (Section 163.04, Florida Statutes) prohibits HOAs from banning solar panels. Your association may have guidelines about placement, but they cannot prevent you from installing solar on your property.
